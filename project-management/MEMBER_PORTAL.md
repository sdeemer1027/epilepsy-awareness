# Purpose

This document defines the complete member experience within the Epilepsy Support Platform (ESP).

The portal is designed to help members manage epilepsy-related information, communicate with caregivers and healthcare professionals, and access trusted educational resources.

---

# Portal Objectives

The Member Portal should enable members to:

- Manage their personal profile.
- Record seizures.
- Track medications.
- Manage appointments.
- Store medical documents.
- Share information with approved caregivers and healthcare professionals.
- Participate in the community.
- Receive reminders and notifications.

# Success Criteria

A member should be able to:

- Complete common daily tasks in under five minutes.
- Find important health information quickly.
- Control who can access their information.
- Feel confident using the platform without technical knowledge.
