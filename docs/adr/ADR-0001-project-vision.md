# ADR-0001: Project Vision

## Status

Accepted

## Date

July 5, 2026

---

# Context

The Epilepsy Support Platform (ESP) is intended to assist individuals living with epilepsy, their caregivers, healthcare professionals, and organizations through education, support, communication, and health management tools.

The platform is not intended to replace an Electronic Health Record (EHR) or hospital information system.

---

# Decision

ESP will function as a **health-support platform** rather than a complete medical records system.

Medical information stored by ESP will be limited to data necessary for personal health management, reporting, and communication between authorized users.

The platform may integrate with external healthcare systems in the future but will not attempt to duplicate their functionality.

---

# Consequences

Benefits include:

- Simpler database design.
- Easier regulatory compliance.
- Better focus on epilepsy management.
- Easier future integrations.
- Reduced maintenance complexity.

Future integrations with healthcare providers remain possible through APIs.
